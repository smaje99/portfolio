## Context and purpose

`it-services-contents-unir` is a personal initiative to improve a template a teacher used to support courses. The goal was to turn it into a more maintainable and reusable foundation for organizing topics, resources, activities, self-assessments, and case studies.

## What I built

Sergio developed the Astro architecture, components, content collections, and deployment. Educational content creation and integrations with other systems are outside the documented contribution.

The public repository documents a composition using Astro, React, MDX, and content collections. The [public demo](https://it-services-contents-unir.vercel.app) provides inspectable evidence, while the [repository](https://github.com/smaje99/it-services-contents-unir) contains the implementation.

## Status

The project retains its **operational** status: the teacher currently uses it to support courses. It is presented as an independent educational project, separate from the data structures set and not as a generalized commercial product.
